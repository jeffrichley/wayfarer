Pre-delivery compliance checks

Written from the map ACX compliance before delivery and its seven decisions. Published 12 Sep; sliced into nine tickets on 14 Sep.

Story coverage

Problem statement

Authors finish a Galley audiobook, upload it to ACX, and wait days to learn a chapter was rejected for loudness, peaks, or missing room tone. Every rejection means another render, another upload, and another wait in the review queue.

Galley already has the audio. It should say what ACX will reject before the author submits.

Solution

Galley checks every rendered chapter against ACX’s audio submission requirements as part of rendering. The Compliance page lists each check per chapter in plain language, with the measured value beside the limit and a jump to the moment that fails.

ACX export stays locked until every blocking check passes.

          Further notes

          ACX’s requirements, as researched on the map: every file between −23 and −18 dB RMS, peaks no higher than −3 dB, a noise floor no higher than −60 dB RMS, 0.5–1 second of room tone at the head and 1–5 seconds at the tail, no file longer than 120 minutes, and opening and closing credits.
